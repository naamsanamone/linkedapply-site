Quick Start Flow

How LinkedApply Pro Works

Three straightforward steps to accelerate your application rate and interview conversion.

1

Configure Your Profile & AI Key

Install the extension, paste your free Google Gemini API key (or OpenAI), and fill out your common recruiter questions in the Question Bank.

2

Build & Tailor Your Resume

Choose an ATS template, import your details, paste your target job description into the Tailor drawer, and generate aligned bullet points.

3

Automate LinkedIn Easy Apply

Search for jobs on LinkedIn, trigger Easy Apply with one click from the popup, and watch LinkedApply Pro fill modals and track your pipeline.

Privacy Architecture

Your Data Stays On Your Machine. Period.

We believe your career information, job history, and AI keys belong exclusively to you.

🔒 100% Local Storage

All resumes, question answers, and job tracking records are saved strictly in your browser's chrome.storage.local.

🚫 No Cloud Database

We operate zero remote servers, backend databases, or middleman proxies. Nothing is ever sent to an external server.

🛡️ No Telemetry or Trackers

Zero Google Analytics, Facebook pixels, or third-party behavioral trackers. Your browsing activity is strictly private.

🔐 Secure HTTPS AI Handshake

Your BYOK keys communicate directly and securely via HTTPS between your browser and the AI provider you configure.
